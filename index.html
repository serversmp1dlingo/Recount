Let's Get Started · Apersepsi

Pernahkah kamu bercerita tentang momen bersama keluarga atau temanmu?

Terkadang kita perlu berbagi momen kita dengan keluarga atau teman. Ketika kita membicarakan pengalaman atau momen di masa lalu, kita menyebutnya bercerita. "Lihat gambar sebuah keluarga di pantai. Jawab empat pertanyaan di bawah ini berdasarkan pengalamanmu sendiri."

A. Let's Build · Penyajian Materi

Apa itu recount text?

Recount text adalah teks yang menceritakan sesuatu yang terjadi di masa lalu. Isinya bisa berupa peristiwa, situasi, atau aktivitas yang terjadi pada hari tertentu. Klik tiap tab untuk menjelajahi materinya.

A recount text is a text that retells events or experiences that happened in the past. The writer tells what happened and the order in which the events occurred.

Contoh judul catatan: "My Holiday" — lalu ditulis urut 1, 2, 3 kejadiannya.

  • Orientation
    Introduces the participants, place, and time of the events.

    Example: Last summer, my family and I went to the beach.

  • Events
    Tells events in the order they happened.

    Example: First, we set up our tent. Then, we went swimming. After that, we had a barbecue.

  • Reorientation
    Gives a comment or feeling about the events.

    Example: It was a wonderful trip and I will never forget it.

  • 1. Past tense verbs
    Events happened in the past.

    went, visited, played, watched, arrived, enjoyed

  • 2. Time connectors
    Show the order of events.

    first, then, next, after that, later, finally

  • 3. Action verbs
    Describe what the participants did.

    swam, cooked, drove, saw, took photos

  • 4. Personal pronouns
    Show who is involved.

    I, we, my, our, me, us

  • 5. Expressions of feeling
    Show the writer's feelings.

    it was exciting, I was happy, it was unforgettable

To retell past events or experiences for informing or entertaining the readers.

      D. Let's Write and Present · Tata Bahasa

      Simple Past Tense in Recount Text

      Study the formula of Simple Past Tense below. Simple Past Tense is used in recount text to describe actions or events that happened and finished in the past.

      ✅ Positive (+) — sentences that state an action
      S + V₂ (past form) + O.
      Example: I visited my grandparents last weekend.
      ❌ Negative (−) — an action did not happen
      S + did not (didn't) + V₁ (base form) + O.
      Example: I didn't go to the beach yesterday.
      ❓ Interrogative (?) — questions about the past
      Did + S + V₁ (base form) + O?
      Example: Did you watch the movie last night?
      Note: In Simple Past Tense, most verbs add -ed at the end (played, visited, cooked). But some verbs are irregular and change form (went, saw, took).

      Summary · Kesimpulan

      Recount Text: Summary

      Recount text is a text that retells events or experiences in the past.

      • 1. Definition: Recount text tells about events or experiences that happened in the past. The purpose is to inform or entertain the reader by retelling what happened.
      • 2. Social function: to inform the reader about past events or experiences; to share and entertain by telling what happened.
      • 3. Generic structure: (1) Orientation — introduces who, when, where. (2) Series of Events — tells the events that happened in order. (3) Reorientation — gives a closing statement and the writer's feelings.
      • 4. Language features: Past tense (went, visited, watched); action verbs (played, cleaned, studied); time connectors (first, then, after that, next, finally); adverbs/adverbials of time (yesterday, last week, in the morning); nouns and pronouns (people, places, things); circumstances of time and place (on Saturday, at the park).
      • 6. Tips for writing recount text: think about the events you want to tell; make sure the events are in the correct order; use past tense and time connectors; end with your feelings or a closing statement.
      • 7. Key points: recount text tells about past events in order; it has three parts (Orientation, Series of Events, Reorientation); it uses past tense, action verbs, and time connectors to make the story clear.
      5. Example of recount text

      Orientation: Last Sunday, I went to my grandmother's house with my family in the morning.

      Series of Events: First, we helped Grandma clean the yard. Then, we picked some vegetables in her garden. After that, Grandma cooked our favorite lunch. In the afternoon, we played board games together. Before we went home, Grandma gave us some fresh fruits from her garden.

      Reorientation: I had a wonderful time with my family. I felt happy and grateful because we could spend quality time together. I hope we can visit Grandma again soon.

      Refleksi akhir: Recount text ada di sekitar kita — dalam diary, cerita liburan, bahkan laporan kegiatan sekolah. Semakin sering kita mengenali strukturnya, semakin mudah kita menulis dan memahami cerita orang lain.
